║  CRC BYTE ORDER (DON'T MESS THIS UP!)                                    ║
╠═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                           ║
║   Modbus uses LITTLE-ENDIAN (low byte first)                             ║
║                                                                           ║
║   ❌ WRONG: (frame[len-2] << 8) | frame[len-1]                           ║
║   ✅ RIGHT:  frame[len-1] << 8  | frame[len-2]                           ║
║                                                                           ║
║   When sending: response[N] = crc & 0xFF;        // Low byte             ║
║                 response[N+1] = (crc >> 8) & 0xFF; // High byte          ║
║                                                                           ║
